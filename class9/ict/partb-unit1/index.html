1. What is the IT-ITeS Industry?

The IT (Information Technology) industry uses computers, software, and networks to process, store, and communicate information. ITeS (IT-enabled Services) refers to services that are delivered using IT infrastructure, such as BPO and KPO operations.

TermFull FormMeaning
ITInformation TechnologyTechnology that uses computers to process data
ITeSIT-enabled ServicesServices provided using IT systems
BPOBusiness Process OutsourcingOutsourcing business tasks to a third party
KPOKnowledge Process OutsourcingOutsourcing knowledge-based tasks
ITES-BPOIT-enabled Services – BPOCustomer support, data entry, back-office work
Memory tip: IT = hardware and software; ITeS = using IT to provide services.

2. The IT Industry in India

India is one of the world's largest IT exporters. Cities like Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Chennai, and NCR are major IT hubs.

Key segments of IT industry

  • IT Services: Software development, consulting, maintenance
  • IT Products: Hardware, software products
  • ITeS / BPO: Call centres, data processing, billing
  • Engineering Services and R&D: Embedded systems, telecom
  • E-Commerce: Online buying/selling platforms

Growth of IT in India

India's IT industry grew due to availability of English-speaking skilled graduates, lower cost of operations, good government policies (like SEZ, STPI), and global demand for IT outsourcing.

Remember: NASSCOM is the trade body representing India's IT-BPM sector.

3. ITeS – IT-enabled Services

ITeS covers services that would not be possible without IT, but whose primary output is a service, not a software product.

TypeExamples
Customer Interaction ServicesCall centres, helpdesks, technical support
Back Office OperationsData entry, data conversion, digitisation
Finance and AccountingPayroll, billing, account management
Healthcare ServicesMedical transcription, medical billing
HR ServicesRecruitment processing, training management
Legal Process Outsourcing (LPO)Legal research, document review

5. Skills Required in IT-ITeS

Technical Skills

  • Basic computer operations (Windows, Linux)
  • Typing speed and accuracy
  • Office productivity tools (Word, Excel, Writer, Calc)
  • Internet and email usage
  • Data entry and data management
  • Knowledge of software tools relevant to the job

Soft Skills

  • Communication (verbal and written)
  • Time management
  • Team work and collaboration
  • Problem-solving attitude
  • Adaptability to changing technology

Key facts about the industry

  • IT-ITeS is one of India's largest employment sectors
  • Industry provides remote work and flexible opportunities
  • Continuous learning is essential due to rapid technology changes

6. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. What is the full form of IT and ITeS? 1 Mark▼
Answer: IT stands for Information Technology. ITeS stands for IT-enabled Services.
Q2. What is BPO? Give two examples. 2 Marks▼
Answer: BPO stands for Business Process Outsourcing. It means outsourcing routine business tasks to a third party using IT. Examples: customer care centres, data entry operations.
Q3. Differentiate between BPO and KPO. 2 Marks▼
Answer: BPO handles routine, rule-based tasks (e.g., customer support, data entry) and requires lower skill levels. KPO handles knowledge-intensive tasks (e.g., research, legal analysis) and requires higher expertise.
Q4. List four career options in the IT-ITeS sector. 2 Marks▼
Answer: Data Entry Operator, Customer Support Executive, Software Developer, Network Administrator.
Q5. Why is India a major destination for IT outsourcing? Give three reasons. 3 Marks▼
Answer: (1) Large pool of English-speaking, educated graduates. (2) Lower operational cost compared to western countries. (3) Government support through SEZ, STPI and favourable IT policies.
Q6. What technical and soft skills are needed in the IT-ITeS sector? 3 Marks▼
Answer: Technical: typing speed, office tools, internet, data management. Soft skills: communication, teamwork, time management, problem-solving.
Q7. Describe any four types of ITeS services. 4 Marks▼
Answer: (1) Customer Interaction Services – call centres, technical support. (2) Back Office Operations – data entry, digitisation. (3) Finance and Accounting – payroll, billing. (4) Healthcare Services – medical transcription, billing.
Quick revision: IT = technology; ITeS = using IT for services; BPO = business tasks outsourced; KPO = knowledge tasks outsourced; India is a global IT hub due to skilled talent and cost advantage.
