1. What is Perception?

Perception is the way a person interprets or makes sense of the world around them based on their senses, experience, and beliefs. Two people can receive the same message but perceive it very differently.

Perception plays a major role in communication because the receiver's perception determines how they understand and respond to the sender's message.

Why Perception Matters

  • Same words can mean different things to different people
  • Background, culture, and experience shape how we interpret messages
  • Misperception is one of the biggest causes of communication breakdown
Example: A glass of water — one person sees it as "half full" (optimistic), another as "half empty" (pessimistic). Same situation, different perspectives.

4. Cultural Perspectives in Communication

Cultural Communication recognises that people from different cultural backgrounds communicate in different ways.

AspectVaries by culture
Eye contactRespectful (Western) vs disrespectful (some Asian cultures)
Physical touchHandshake (formal Western) vs Namaste (Indian)
SilenceAgreement (some cultures) vs discomfort (others)
Personal spaceClose (Middle East) vs more distance (Northern Europe)
DirectnessDirect/blunt (German) vs indirect/polite (Japanese)
TimePunctuality very important (UK/USA) vs flexible (some cultures)

Tips for cross-cultural communication

  • Learn about the other culture before communicating
  • Avoid slang and idioms that may not translate well
  • Be patient and respectful of differences
  • Confirm understanding through feedback
Important in IT-ITeS: Indian IT professionals often communicate with clients from USA, UK, Europe and Australia. Cross-cultural communication skills are essential in this industry.

AggressiveExpressing opinions forcefully without regard for others' feelings"You're wrong and I don't care what you think."

Assertive communication is the recommended style in professional environments — it is honest, direct and respectful.

7. Professional Communication Etiquette

Professional etiquette means following expected standards of behaviour when communicating in a work environment.

Telephone Etiquette

  • Answer calls promptly (within 3 rings)
  • Greet with name and company: "Good morning, this is Rahul from XYZ."
  • Speak clearly, politely and professionally
  • Do not put caller on hold for a long time without checking in
  • End call politely: "Thank you for calling. Have a good day."

Email Etiquette

  • Write a clear and specific subject line
  • Begin with a proper salutation (Dear Mr./Ms.)
  • Keep the message concise and to the point
  • Proofread before sending — check spelling and grammar
  • End with a courteous closing (Regards, Yours sincerely)
  • Reply within 24 hours in a professional setting

Meeting Etiquette

  • Be on time (arrive 5 minutes early)
  • Come prepared — read agenda beforehand
  • Listen carefully; do not interrupt
  • Stay on topic
  • Take notes
  • Switch phones to silent

8. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. What is perception? How does it affect communication? 2 Marks▼
Perception is how a person interprets the world based on their experience, senses and beliefs. It affects communication because the same message can be understood differently by different people based on their perceptions, causing misunderstandings.
Q2. Name any four factors that affect communication. 2 Marks▼
Visual perception, language/vocabulary, past experience, prejudice/bias, feelings and emotions, environment, culture, gender. (Any four.)
Q3. Name the eight parts of speech with one example each. 4 Marks▼
Noun (school), Pronoun (he), Verb (run), Adjective (tall), Adverb (quickly), Preposition (in), Conjunction (and), Interjection (Wow!).
Q4. What are the four kinds of sentences? Give an example of each. 2 Marks▼
Declarative – "She studies well." | Interrogative – "Where is the library?" | Imperative – "Open your books." | Exclamatory – "What a great performance!"
Q5. Differentiate between assertive, aggressive and passive communication. 3 Marks▼
Assertive = expressing needs clearly and respectfully (ideal). Aggressive = expressing forcefully without regard for others. Passive = avoiding expressing opinions; giving in too easily.
Q6. Why is cultural awareness important in communication? 2 Marks▼
Different cultures have different norms for eye contact, gestures, personal space and directness. Without cultural awareness, actions or words that are polite in one culture may be offensive in another, causing miscommunication.
Q7. State any four rules of email etiquette. 2 Marks▼
(1) Write a clear subject line. (2) Begin with a proper salutation. (3) Keep the message concise and error-free. (4) End with a courteous closing. (5) Reply within 24 hours. (Any four.)
