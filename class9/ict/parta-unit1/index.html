TopicKey Points
3 Methods of CommunicationVerbal (words), Non-verbal (body language), Visual (images/signs)
Non-verbal typesFacial expression, eye contact, gesture, posture, paralanguage, personal space
Communication CycleSender → Encoding → Channel → Receiver → Decoding → Feedback (→ back to Sender)
8 Cycle ElementsSender, Message, Encoding, Channel, Receiver, Decoding, Feedback, Noise
7 C's of CommunicationClear, Concise, Correct, Complete, Courteous, Coherent, Concrete
Types of NoisePhysical, Semantic, Psychological, Physiological
Parts of SpeechNoun, Pronoun, Verb, Adjective, Adverb, Preposition, Conjunction, Interjection
4 Sentence TypesDeclarative, Interrogative, Imperative, Exclamatory
Formal Letter partsSender Address, Date, Receiver Address, Salutation, Subject, Body, Closing, Signature
Notice — 5 W'sWho, What, When, Where, Why
Assertive styleClear + confident + respectful — ideal for professional communication
Writing ProcessPre-write → Draft → Revise → Edit → Publish
