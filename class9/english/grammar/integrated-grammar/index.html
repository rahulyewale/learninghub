3. Active and Passive Voice

Active Voice: The subject performs the action.

Passive Voice: The subject receives the action.

ActivePassive
The teacher praises the student.The student is praised by the teacher.
He wrote a letter.A letter was written by him.

4. Reported Speech

Reported speech tells what someone said without using the exact words.

  • Direct: She said, “I am tired.”
  • Indirect: She said that she was tired.
Rule: Change pronouns, tense, and time expressions where needed.

6. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Fill in the blank: She ____ (go) to school every day. 1 Mark
Answer: goes
Q2. Rewrite in passive voice: The boy kicked the ball. 2 Marks
Answer: The ball was kicked by the boy.
Q3. What is the use of 'must'? 1 Mark
Answer: 'Must' is used to show necessity or obligation.
Q4. Change into indirect speech: He said, “I am late.” 2 Marks
Answer: He said that he was late.
Q5. Identify the subordinate clause: I will wait until you arrive. 2 Marks
Answer: until you arrive

7. Quick Revision Tips

  • Always check tense clues in the sentence
  • Use correct subject-verb agreement
  • Modal + base verb
  • Voice changes the focus, not the meaning
  • Reported speech often changes time and pronouns
