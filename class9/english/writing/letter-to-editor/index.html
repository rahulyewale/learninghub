1. What is a Letter to the Editor?

It is a formal letter written to a newspaper editor to express opinions, raise awareness about public issues, or request action regarding a problem.

Format

PartWhat to include
Sender’s addressTop left corner
DateBelow the address
Editor’s addressNewspaper office address
SubjectShort and specific
SalutationSir / Madam
BodyIntroduction, issue, suggestions, conclusion
ClosingYours faithfully / sincerely
NameSender’s full name

2. Important Writing Points

  • Keep the tone formal and polite
  • Mention the issue clearly
  • Give facts, not emotions only
  • Suggest practical solutions
  • Use proper paragraphing
Useful themes: Pollution, bad roads, water shortage, increasing traffic, waste disposal, or noise pollution.

3. Sample Letter

72, Green Park
New Delhi
20 June 2026

The Editor
The Times of India
New Delhi

Subject: Rising Air Pollution in Our City

Sir/Madam,
I wish to draw your attention to the alarming rise in air pollution in our city. Vehicles, construction dust and burning of waste have made the air unhealthy, especially for children and the elderly. The authorities should increase checks on vehicles, control construction dust and promote public transport. Citizens should also avoid littering and burning garbage.

I hope you will highlight this issue in your newspaper.

Yours faithfully,
Aarav Sharma

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. What is the purpose of a letter to the editor? 1 Mark
Answer: It is written to express opinions or raise public issues through a newspaper editor.
Q2. Mention two common topics for this letter. 2 Marks
Answer: Pollution, bad roads, water shortage, or waste disposal are common topics.
Q3. What tone should be used in the letter? 1 Mark
Answer: The tone should be formal, polite and respectful.
Q4. Why is it important to suggest solutions? 2 Marks
Answer: A letter to the editor should not only point out a problem but also offer practical suggestions for improvement.

5. Quick Revision

  • Formal letter to newspaper editor
  • Use issue + facts + suggestions
  • Be polite and specific
  • End with Yours faithfully/sincerely
