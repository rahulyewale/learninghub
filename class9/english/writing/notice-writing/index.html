1. What is a Notice?

A notice is a short, formal written announcement used to inform a large group of people about an event, meeting, activity, lost item, or important information.

Standard Format

PartWhat to write
NoticeCentered at the top
School/Organisation NameHeading
DateLeft-aligned
Title / SubjectShort, clear title
BodyWhat, when, where, who, and how
Writer’s NameSignature line
Designatione.g., Student Secretary
Remember: A notice should be brief, clear, and written in formal language.

2. Rules for Writing a Good Notice

  • Use simple, formal language
  • Keep it short and to the point
  • Mention date, time, venue, and purpose clearly
  • Write in the past or future tense appropriately
  • Use a proper heading and signature

3. Sample Notice

NOTICE
Green Valley Public School
Date: 20 June 2026

Inter-House Debate Competition
This is to inform all students of Classes 8 and 9 that an Inter-House Debate Competition will be held on 30 June 2026 in the school auditorium at 10:00 a.m. Interested students must give their names to their class teachers by 25 June 2026.

Ananya Sharma
Student Secretary

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. What is a notice? 1 Mark
Answer: A notice is a short formal announcement used to inform many people about an event or important information.
Q2. Name the main parts of a notice. 2 Marks
Answer: Notice, school/organisation name, date, title, body, name and designation of the writer.
Q3. Why should a notice be brief? 2 Marks
Answer: A notice is meant for quick reading by many people, so it should be short, clear and direct.
Q4. Write one important rule for notice writing. 1 Mark
Answer: It should mention the date, time, venue and purpose clearly.

5. Quick Revision

  • Notice = short formal announcement
  • Use who/what/when/where/why
  • Include writer’s name and designation
  • Keep language simple and formal
