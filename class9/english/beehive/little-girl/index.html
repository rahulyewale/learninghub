📘 Class 9 English — Beehive: The Little Girl

NCERT study notes, characters, theme and exam questions

SummaryThemesCharactersQuestions

1. Chapter Summary

The story shows the relationship between Kezia and her father. Kezia feels afraid of him because he is strict and emotionally distant. But after seeing how tired and burdened he is, she begins to understand him better. The story ends by showing her deep need for love and security.

Core idea: What looks like harshness on the outside may hide love, responsibility, and stress.

2. Themes

ThemeExplanation
Parent-child relationshipThe gap between Kezia and her father gradually reduces
Fear and misunderstandingKezia mistakes her father’s strictness for cruelty
Compassion and empathyShe later sees his hard work and lonely life

3. Characters

Kezia

A sensitive child who longs for affection and feels lonely. Her fear changes into understanding.

Kezia’s father

Strict, busy, tired and emotionally reserved. He loves his daughter but struggles to express it.

Kezia’s mother & grandmother

The mother is less central, while the grandmother provides comfort and warmth.

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Why was Kezia afraid of her father? 2 Marks
Answer: She was afraid because he was strict, spoke in a rough tone, and did not show much affection.
Q2. How did Kezia’s view of her father change? 3 Marks
Answer: She first saw him as harsh and frightening. But later, when she saw him tired and lonely, she understood his responsibilities and began to feel sympathy and love for him.
Q3. What message does the story give about parents? 4 Marks
Answer: Parents may appear strict because of stress and responsibilities, but they still love their children. Children should try to understand them with patience and empathy.
Q4. What role does the grandmother play? 2 Marks
Answer: She gives Kezia comfort, warmth and emotional support. She is a source of affection in Kezia’s life.

5. Quick Revision

  • Fear changes into understanding
  • Strictness may hide love
  • Children need affection and reassurance
  • Use empathy in character-based answers
