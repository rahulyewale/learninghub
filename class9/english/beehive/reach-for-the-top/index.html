NCERT study notes on Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova

SummaryTwo PartsThemesQuestions

1. Chapter Summary

This chapter is about two inspiring women achievers: Santosh Yadav, the first woman in the world to climb Mount Everest twice, and Maria Sharapova, a Russian tennis champion who achieved success through hard work, sacrifice and determination.

Main message: Great success comes from discipline, courage, sacrifice, and self-belief.

2. The Two Parts

Santosh Yadav

  • Born in a conservative family in Haryana
  • Defied social restrictions to study and train
  • Showed courage, determination and concern for others
  • Climbed Everest twice

Maria Sharapova

  • Born in Russia, moved to the USA for training
  • Lived away from her mother during early training
  • Worked hard despite loneliness and struggle
  • Became a world-famous tennis player

3. Themes

ThemeExplanation

Use in answers: Mention achievements, struggle, and the values they represent.

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Who were the two achievers discussed in the chapter? 1 Mark
Answer: Santosh Yadav and Maria Sharapova.
Q2. What made Santosh Yadav unique? 2 Marks
Answer: Santosh Yadav became the first woman in the world to climb Mount Everest twice. She achieved this through courage, discipline and determination.
Q3. How did Maria Sharapova achieve success? 3 Marks
Answer: Maria Sharapova left Russia at a young age, trained hard in the USA, lived separately from her mother, and faced loneliness and sacrifice. Her dedication and hard work made her a world champion.
Q4. What common qualities do both women share? 3 Marks
Answer: Both women are determined, courageous, hardworking, and willing to sacrifice comfort for success. They also serve as role models for young people.
Q5. Write the moral message of the chapter. 4 Marks
Answer: The chapter teaches that success does not come easily. It needs courage, hard work, discipline, sacrifice, and the confidence to follow one's dream despite obstacles.

5. Quick Revision

  • Two inspiring achievers: Santosh and Maria
  • Focus on courage + hard work + sacrifice
  • Use comparison in long answers
  • Women empowerment is an important idea
