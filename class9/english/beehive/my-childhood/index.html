1. Chapter Summary

My Childhood is an autobiographical chapter by Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. It describes his early life in Rameswaram, his humble family background, his supportive parents, and the people who influenced his values and dreams.

Main ideas

  • Kalam grew up in a simple but disciplined household.
  • His father taught him honesty, spirituality, and self-respect.
  • His mother taught him kindness, patience, and sharing.
  • He had Hindu friends and respected elders from different communities.
  • The chapter highlights unity, tolerance, and hard work.
Remember: This chapter is not just about childhood; it shows how values from home shape a great personality.

Character sketch: Kalam's father

He was wise, calm, spiritual, and practical. He believed in dignity, not wealth. He taught Kalam that great things can be achieved without greed or fear.

Character sketch: Kalam's mother

She was affectionate, caring, and generous. She fed many people and taught the importance of sharing and kindness.

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Who is the author of My Childhood? 1 Mark
Answer: The author is Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam.
Q2. What values did Kalam learn from his parents? 2 Marks
Answer: From his father, he learned honesty, discipline, spirituality and self-respect. From his mother, he learned kindness, compassion and the habit of sharing.
Q3. How does the chapter show communal harmony? 3 Marks
Answer: The people of Rameswaram of different religions lived together peacefully. Kalam’s Hindu friends respected his family, and his father had good relations with elders from other communities. This shows unity and mutual respect.
Q4. Describe Kalam’s father in one paragraph. 4 Marks
Answer: Kalam’s father was a wise and spiritually inclined man. He led a simple life, believed in honesty and dignity, and gave his son the freedom to grow with moral strength. He was calm, disciplined, and deeply respected by the village people.
Q5. Why is this chapter important for students? 4 Marks
Answer: The chapter teaches that background or wealth does not define success. Strong values, hard work, respect for others, and self-belief can shape a successful life.
