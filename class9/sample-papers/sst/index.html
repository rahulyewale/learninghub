SST Sample Paper (40 Marks)

Portions: Geography Ch 4 Climate, Civics Ch 1 Democracy

Q1. Define climate.
Answer: Long-term average weather of a place.
Explanation: Measured over years, not days.
Q2. Two differences: weather vs climate.
Answer: Weather short-term; climate long-term pattern.
Explanation: Time period is key difference.
Q3. Two features of democracy.
Answer: Elected government, free and fair elections.
Explanation: Ensures public participation and accountability.
Q4. Why monsoon important for India?
Answer: Supports farming, water supply, economy.
Explanation: Major rainfall comes from monsoon.
Q5. Explain four seasons of India.
Answer: Cold, Hot, SW Monsoon, Retreating Monsoon with month ranges and characteristics.
Explanation: Seasonal changes follow wind reversal pattern.
Q6. Why democracy is better?
Answer: Protects rights, allows choice, accountability, peaceful power change.
Explanation: Citizens can correct government through elections.
