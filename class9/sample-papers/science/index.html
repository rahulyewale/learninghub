Science Sample Paper (40 Marks)

Chapters: Is Matter Around Us Pure, Fundamental Unit of Life, Motion

Q1. Milk is a?
Answer: Colloid
Explanation: It has dispersed particles.
Q2. Powerhouse of cell?
Answer: Mitochondria
Explanation: Releases energy.
Q3. SI unit of acceleration?
Answer: m/s2
Explanation: Change in velocity per second.
Q4. Define osmosis.
Answer: Movement of water through semipermeable membrane from high to low water concentration.
Explanation: Driven by concentration difference.
Q5. Write 3 equations of motion.
Answer: v=u+at, s=ut+1/2at2, v2=u2+2as.
Explanation: For uniform acceleration.
Q6. Compare solution, colloid, suspension.
Answer: Solution homogeneous; colloid shows Tyndall effect; suspension settles and can be filtered.
Explanation: Particle size and behavior differ.
