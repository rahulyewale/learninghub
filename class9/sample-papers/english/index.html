English Sample Paper (40 Marks)

Portions: My Childhood, The Little Girl, Reach for the Top, Notice, Letter to Editor

Q1. Fill: She ____ (write) now.
Answer: is writing
Explanation: Present continuous with “now”.
Q2. Change to indirect: He said, “I am tired.”
Answer: He said that he was tired.
Explanation: Present shifts to past.
Q3. Why did Kezia fear her father?
Answer: He appeared strict and less expressive.
Explanation: Her understanding changes later.
Q4. Write a notice for cleanliness drive.
Answer: Formal notice with date, event, venue, timings, authority name.
Explanation: Keep format and clarity.
Q5. Letter to editor on plastic waste.
Answer: Formal structure with problem, effects, suggestions, closing.
Explanation: Include practical solutions.
