1. What is Climate?

Climate is the average weather condition of a place over a long period of time, usually about 30 years.

Weather changes day to day, but climate describes the long-term pattern.

Easy difference: Weather = today’s condition; Climate = usual condition over years.

2. Factors Affecting the Climate of India

FactorHow it affects climate
LatitudeIndia lies in the tropical and sub-tropical zone, so it receives high temperatures.
AltitudeHigher places are cooler than lowlands.
Pressure and wind systemControls monsoon winds and rainfall.
Distance from seaCoastal regions have moderate climate; interior areas have extremes.
Ocean currents / reliefMountains block winds and influence rainfall.

3. The Monsoon

India’s climate is dominated by the monsoon, a seasonal wind system that changes direction with the seasons and brings rainfall.

Why does India get monsoon rains?

  • Uneven heating of land and sea
  • Shift of the ITCZ (Inter-Tropical Convergence Zone)
  • Movement of jet streams
  • Role of the Himalayas and Tibetan Plateau
Important: Southwest monsoon brings most of India’s rainfall between June and September.

4. Seasons in India

SeasonMonthsCharacteristics
Cold weather seasonDec-FebCool and dry weather; low temperatures in north India
Hot weather seasonMar-MayHigh temperature, loo, dust storms
Southwest monsoon seasonJun-SepRainfall across most parts of India
Retreating monsoon seasonOct-NovMonsoon withdraws; cyclones in east coast

Distribution of rainfall

Rainfall is not uniform in India. The western coast, northeastern India, and the Himalayan foothills receive heavy rainfall, while Rajasthan and parts of the Deccan Plateau receive less rain.

5. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Define climate. 1 Mark
Answer: Climate is the average weather condition of a place over a long period of time.
Q2. Differentiate between weather and climate. 2 Marks
Answer: Weather changes frequently over short periods, while climate is the average condition of weather over a long period, usually about 30 years.
Q3. Why is the monsoon important for India? 2 Marks
Answer: The monsoon provides most of India’s rainfall and is crucial for agriculture, water supply and the economy.
Q4. Name the four seasons of India. 2 Marks
Answer: Cold weather season, hot weather season, southwest monsoon season and retreating monsoon season.
Q5. State any three factors affecting the climate of India. 3 Marks
Answer: Latitude, altitude, distance from sea, pressure and wind system, and relief are major factors affecting climate.

6. Quick Revision Tips

  • Climate = long-term weather pattern
  • Monsoon = seasonal wind system
  • Southwest monsoon gives most rainfall
  • Coastal areas have moderate climate
  • Four seasons are easy to remember in order
