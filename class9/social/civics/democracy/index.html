1. What is Democracy?

Democracy is a form of government in which the rulers are elected by the people.

It is based on the idea of popular participation, equality and accountability.

Simple meaning: Government of the people, by the people, for the people.

2. Features of Democracy

FeatureMeaning
Free and fair electionsPeople choose their representatives through regular elections
Universal adult franchiseEvery adult citizen has the right to vote
Rule of lawEveryone is equal before the law
AccountabilityGovernment must answer to the people
Rights and freedomsCitizens enjoy basic rights

3. Why Democracy?

  • It allows people to choose their government
  • It improves the dignity and equality of citizens
  • It gives people the right to criticize and remove bad rulers
  • It encourages participation and discussion

Challenges to democracy

  • Corruption
  • Pressure groups and money power
  • Lack of awareness among citizens
  • Misuse of power by rulers
Important: Democracy may have problems, but it is still better than rule without people's consent.

4. Sample Questions with Answers

Q1. Define democracy. 1 Mark
Answer: Democracy is a form of government in which the rulers are elected by the people.
Q2. Write two features of democracy. 2 Marks
Answer: Free and fair elections and universal adult franchise are two important features of democracy.
Q3. Why is democracy considered better than non-democratic rule? 3 Marks
Answer: Democracy gives people the right to choose leaders, ensures equality before law, and makes the government accountable to citizens.
Q4. State any two challenges faced by democracy. 2 Marks
Answer: Corruption and misuse of money or power are common challenges faced by democracy.
Q5. What is universal adult franchise? 2 Marks
Answer: It means every adult citizen has the right to vote without discrimination.

5. Quick Revision Tips
